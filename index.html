Local Law 97

NYC's Carbon Law Is Already in Effect

Local Law 97 sets building-level carbon intensity limits that tighten in 2030 and again in 2035. Buildings over 25,000 sq ft that exceed their limits face annual fines of $268 per excess metric ton of CO₂e — fines that compound every year.

We model your building's current carbon intensity, project performance against upcoming limits, and design the most cost-effective compliance pathway — whether that's HVAC upgrades, electrification, or purchasing RECs.
